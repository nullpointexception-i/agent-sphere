Business System Mock (drawer mount)

Simulates a third-party business system (for example, a Human Resource Intermediary) embedding the AgentSphere widget in a right-side drawer.

Click "AI Assistant" in the top-right or the floating button in the bottom-right to open the drawer and mount the widget with mountTo; closing the drawer calls unmount. It opens automatically when ?otc=/?error= is present.

Dev config: provider: business, autoLogin: true (mock IdP :9000).
